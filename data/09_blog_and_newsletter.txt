ARTICLE: Beyond standard Google Dataform — Smart templates to save BigQuery costs and Dev time
Published: October 6, 2025 (English)
Bolaji describes how he extended Google Dataform beyond its basic capabilities by building a custom JavaScript-based template framework at Gozem. The framework automates the incremental data flow: table existence checks, staging, column comparison, dynamic partition extraction, partition-filtered merges, and scoped assertions. This approach achieved fully native data transformations inside BigQuery without external scripts or wasteful full-table scans, resulting in significant cost savings and faster development cycles.

ARTICLE: Where to find data for a national study
Published: November 6, 2021 (French)
A guide to finding national-level study data, using Benin as an example. Covers data sources including INStaD, NADA portal, Benin Data Portal, Afrobarometre, World Bank, DHS Program, ILOstat, and UNdata. Notes that Benin ranked 83rd globally on the Open Data Watch 2020 inventory with a score of 52/100.
