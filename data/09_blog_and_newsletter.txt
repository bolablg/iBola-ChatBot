ARTICLE: Beyond standard Google Dataform - Smart templates to save BigQuery costs and Dev time
Published: October 6, 2025 (English)
Bolaji describes how he extended Google Dataform beyond its basic capabilities by building a custom JavaScript-based template framework at Gozem. The framework automates the incremental data flow: table existence checks, staging, column comparison, dynamic partition extraction, partition-filtered merges, and scoped assertions. This approach achieved fully native data transformations inside BigQuery without external scripts or wasteful full-table scans, resulting in significant cost savings and faster development cycles. This smart-templates craft contributed to his FinOps results at Gozem.
