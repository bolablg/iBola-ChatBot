iSHEERO is a visionary initiative led by experts to promote data science and artificial intelligence in Benin and across Francophone Africa. Our mission is built on three powerful pillars:
- Empower, Guide, and Inspire: We equip young Beninese talent with cutting-edge AI skills, fostering innovation and creating new opportunities in the field of artificial intelligence.
- Build a Local Ecosystem: We are establishing a robust data science ecosystem, positioning Benin as a hub for international AI projects and innovation.
- Champion AI in Benin: We help businesses discover and embrace the transformative power of AI to enhance their operational performance and drive growth.
