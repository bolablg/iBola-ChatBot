### Gozem - Ecommerce Recommendation System
At Gozem, I worked with the product team to deploy a recommendation system in our user app for our e-commerce business line. After the deployment, I was responsible for tracking the performance of this new feature. To achieve this, I defined the following events and worked with developers to track related data points:
* User Interaction Events: Capturing clicks, views, and interactions with recommended products.
* Purchase Events: Tracking conversions and purchases resulting from recommendations.
* Feedback Events: Monitoring user ratings and reviews on recommended products.
In my post-mortem analysis review, I presented the following performance indicators:
* Click-Through Rate (CTR): Measuring the percentage of users who click on recommended products.
* Conversion Rate: Tracking the percentage of users who purchase recommended products.
* User Satisfaction: Analyzing user feedback and ratings to gauge the quality of recommendations.
By defining these events and success metrics, I enabled the team to optimize the recommendation system, enhance user experience, and drive higher engagement and sales.

### Gozem - Recommendation A/B Test
We performed an experiment for the recommendation system to evaluate its effectiveness. In addition to event definition and metrics calculation, I was responsible for the following:
1. Test Design: Collaborated with the product team to define the test hypothesis and objectives. We aimed to determine if personalized recommendations would increase user engagement and conversions.
2. Implementation: Worked with developers to implement the A/B test, ensuring that users were randomly assigned to either the control group (no recommendations) or the test group (with personalized recommendations).
3. Data Analysis: Analyzed the collected data to compare the performance of both groups, focusing on metrics such as click-through rate (CTR), conversion rate, and user satisfaction.
The results showed a significant increase in CTR and conversion rates for the test group, along with a similar level of positive user feedback as the control group. Based on these insights, the product team rolled out the personalized recommendation system to all users. My team continuously monitors and refines the recommendation algorithms to maintain high engagement and satisfaction.
The A/B test provided actionable insights that led to data-driven decisions, ultimately driving higher sales.
