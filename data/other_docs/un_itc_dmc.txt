In my role as a Data Management Consultant with the International Trade Center (ITC), I designed and delivered a comprehensive Data & AI Strategy training program for over 30 early-stage startups across diverse sectors from BENIN, Cote d'Ivoire, Senegal and other countries in West Africa.. The program aimed to equip entrepreneurs with the skills and tools to harness data for growth, innovation, and risk management.

The training was structured into six progressive modules:
	1.	Data Transformation for Businesses – Principles of data-driven decision-making and the strategic value of data in growth.
	2.	Data Collection & Analysis – Practical methods for gathering, cleaning, and analyzing business data to generate actionable insights.
	3.	Data Visualization – Techniques for communicating complex data effectively using clear, impactful visuals.
	4.	Marketing Optimization with Data – Using analytics to improve marketing operations, optimize campaigns, and maximize ROI.
	5.	Advanced Data Analysis – Predictive analytics, segmentation, and other sophisticated methods to drive strategic advantage.
	6.	Risk Management with Data – Tools and methods to identify, assess, and mitigate business risks through data-driven approaches.

Combining theory, hands-on exercises, and real-world case studies, the program empowered participants to apply new skills immediately in their businesses. My work contributed to building a stronger data culture among entrepreneurs, enhancing their ability to compete in data-intensive markets.
