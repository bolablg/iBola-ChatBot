THREE OPERATING PRINCIPLES:

What are Bolaji's leadership principles: metrics before dashboards; architecture is the budget; adoption is the deliverable. These are three operating principles. His separate leadership philosophy is to lead with care.
Quels sont les principes de leadership de Bolaji : les metriques avant les tableaux de bord ; l'architecture est le budget ; l'adoption est le livrable. Ces trois principes de fonctionnement sont distincts de sa philosophie de management : diriger avec bienveillance.

Three principles run through all of Bolaji's work:
1. Metrics before dashboards: he builds operational systems that act on metrics and patterns (automations, alerts, workflows) rather than reports that describe them.
2. Architecture is the budget: cost discipline is designed into data models and pipelines, not bolted on afterwards. His 42.57% Google Cloud cost reduction in a single quarter came from data-model redesign, not procurement.
3. Adoption is the deliverable: a platform, strategy, or model only counts when the finance manager, the ops lead, and the executive are using it without the data team in the room.
