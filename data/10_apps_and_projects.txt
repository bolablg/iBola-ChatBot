iBOLA — AI-POWERED PORTFOLIO CHATBOT
What: A production-grade multi-agent RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation) chatbot that serves as Bolaji's professional portfolio assistant. Users can ask questions about his experience, skills, education, and projects.
Stack: LangChain, LangGraph, Google Gemini, ChromaDB, FastAPI, Python, Docker, Google Cloud Run.
Architecture: Agentic workflow with guardrail scoring, hybrid search (BM25 + vector MMR + RRF fusion), document grading, query rewriting, and structured LLM outputs. Supports SSE streaming.
Live at: chat.bolablg.com
Outcome: Demonstrates Bolaji's ability to build production AI systems end-to-end, from data pipeline to deployment.
