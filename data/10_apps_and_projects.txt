iBOLA - AI-POWERED PORTFOLIO CHATBOT
What: a production-grade agentic RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation) chatbot that serves as Bolaji's professional portfolio assistant. Users can ask questions about his experience, skills, education, and projects.
Stack: LangGraph, Google Gemini, ChromaDB, FastAPI, Python, Docker, Google Cloud Run.
Architecture: agentic workflow with guardrail scoring, hybrid search (BM25 + vector MMR + RRF fusion), document grading, query rewriting, and structured LLM outputs. Supports SSE streaming.
Live at: chat.bolablg.com
Outcome: demonstrates Bolaji's ability to build production AI systems end to end, from data pipeline to deployment. His website's experience pages cite this chatbot pattern as a use case.
