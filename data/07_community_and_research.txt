BOLAJI BALOGOUN - COMMUNITY LEADERSHIP, MENTORSHIP & RESEARCH

Bolaji is actively involved in community building, mentorship, and AI research for African languages. He has helped build the African data and AI ecosystem beyond his industry roles.

---

FOUNDING VICE-CHAIR - iSHEERO (December 2021 - Present)
Website: isheero.com

iSheero is an initiative promoting data science and artificial intelligence in Benin and across Francophone Africa. Bolaji is a FOUNDING Vice-Chair.

Three pillars of iSheero:
1. Skills development: equipping young Beninese talent with AI and data science skills through workshops, mentorship, and hands-on projects.
2. Local ecosystem: building a data science ecosystem to position Benin as a hub for international AI projects.
3. AI in business: helping companies adopt AI in their operating workflows.

Bolaji's contributions as founding Vice-Chair:
- Led a real-time visualization lab in Google Cloud at the Africa TechUp Tour bootcamp.
- Engaged with DataCamp scholarship recipients in data engineering as part of an iSheero partnership program.
- Served as a review contributor to Benin's National AI and Big Data Strategy with the Ministry of Digital Affairs.
- Co-trained directors of information systems (DSIs) across Benin's Ministry of Finance departments in data science.

---

CHAIR & CO-FOUNDER - TAKWIMU LAB (2019 - 2022)

Bolaji co-founded and chaired Takwimu LAB, a research collective advancing NLP for low-resource African languages, built as a community for AI and Data Science learners in Francophone Africa.

Key achievements:
- Helped collect, clean, annotate, and release over 70,000 aligned French-Fongbe and French-Ewe sentence pairs on Zenodo under the UNESCO-backed AI4D program.
- Built a prototype English-Fongbe Neural Machine Translation app through the AI4D-Africa Network with UNESCO support.
- Won the Zindi Africa Award for the AI4D African Dataset Challenge in 2020.
- Co-organized a machine translation challenge on Zindi Africa.
- Published the parallel text dataset on Zenodo (zenodo.org/record/4266935, November 2020).
- Contributed to the paper "Participatory Research for Low-resourced Machine Translation", presented at the AfricaNLP Workshop at the EACL 2021 conference (arXiv:2104.02516).

This work positioned Bolaji at the intersection of AI research and African language preservation, contributing to multilingual NLP for underrepresented languages.
