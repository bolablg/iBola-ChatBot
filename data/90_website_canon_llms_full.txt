# Bolaji BALOGOUN · Biography

*Staff Data Engineer · Data Architect.*

Former Head of Data and Data Director with over seven years building production data pipelines and platforms, backed by a decade across data science and advanced analytics. Bolaji combines Staff-level engineering depth with experience leading teams and data functions, from cloud migration and high-volume pipelines to operational data products, governance, and cost-disciplined architecture.

## Platform engineering and leadership

Bolaji was Gozem’s first data hire. As Global Data Analyst, he built the analytical function from the ground up, migrated tens of terabytes to GCP and BigQuery through 50+ pipelines in one quarter without interruption, and delivered foundations for operations, finance, marketing, and fraud-pattern monitoring. He later led the data function as Head of Data, growing the team from 4 to 14+ and owning governance, FinOps, analytics engineering, and fintech data products. As Data Director through July 2026, he owned the data function end to end and co-led Gozem’s AI strategy.

## Data products at operating scale

Bolaji built and deployed the [Gozem Data Hub](https://data.gozem.co), a governed platform serving 650+ employees, field agents, and partners across five African operating countries, with Singapore as headquarters. He built the initial platform in under one month and personally coded 17 of its 19 microservices, designed database schemas and persistence models, and onboarded engineers who shipped additional self-service applications. The platform combined React, FastAPI, Nginx, MongoDB, Redis, NATS, BigQuery, Google Cloud Storage, Docker Compose, Artifact Registry, and shared Python utilities.

The Data Hub powered application-serving pipelines, operational workflows, data exploration, and self-service analytics beyond dashboards. Bolaji implemented centralized authentication and routing, JWT and API-key access, scoped role-based permissions, service-to-service tokens, OTP and refresh-token hashing, and rate limiting. He also set up health checks, retries, stale-execution recovery, rollback controls, targeted test suites, security checks, and GitLab CI/CD gates. The work supported 250+ pipelines with quality checks, lineage, access controls, and service-level monitoring.

## Applied AI, automation, and reliability

Bolaji co-led Gozem’s AI strategy and shipped 30+ production AI and automation tools across document intelligence, RAG support, fraud alerts, monitoring, and agentic workflows. An invoice extraction workflow processed thousands of paper invoices and reduced the work from one to one and a half weeks to less than one day, saving approximately 40 hours per month for each Financing Manager. A real-time market monitoring program maintained an 88% rider match rate during a 48% demand surge and supported a 10.2% peak-event supply-rate lift.

He also built application-ready data flows from MongoDB, BigQuery, Google Cloud Storage, and Sheets, plus a near-real-time MongoDB CDC pipeline that published change events through Pub/Sub, processed them in Dataflow, updated BigQuery, and supported real-time visualization in Looker Studio. These systems paired data products with operational services and decision workflows.

## FinOps and analytical serving

As Head of Data, Bolaji redesigned Dataform dependencies, incremental models, shared marts, partitions, MERGE patterns, and assertions. He introduced job-metadata categorization and cost monitoring that reduced Google Cloud costs by 42.57% in one quarter while preserving data freshness and integrity. He also deployed Metabase and ClickHouse on AWS EC2, then used Airflow to replicate selected BigQuery tables and partitions into ClickHouse with use-case-specific history windows of three to 18 months for lower-cost analytical serving.

## Earlier work and advisory practice

At Rintio, Bolaji designed a Hadoop and Apache Spark data lake with Elasticsearch, processed more than 100 GB of agricultural data, and developed crop-yield forecast models with R² above 80% in most cases. He delivered an executive data and AI training mission for Société Générale Maroc.

As a Data Management Consultant with the International Trade Centre, he designed reusable data-collection and management materials, trained 15 ECOWAS startups, and coached five founders. He co-founded Takwimu LAB, whose low-resource African-language NLP work won the AI4D Africa Award in 2020 and reached the AfricaNLP workshop at EACL 2021. He is also founding Vice-Chair of [iSheero](https://www.isheero.com/), where he designed and delivered a hands-on Google Cloud streaming module using Pub/Sub, Dataflow, BigQuery, and Looker Studio, and contributed to the review of Benin’s national AI and Big Data strategy.
