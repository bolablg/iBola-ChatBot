# Bolaji BALOGOUN · Biography

*Data Engineer · Data Architect.*

Bolaji is a Data Engineer, Data Architect, and former Head of Data based in Little Rock, Arkansas, United States. His work spans Google Cloud data platforms, marketplace analytics, and data leadership, including strategy, technical roadmaps, governance, and FinOps.

## Platform engineering and leadership

Bolaji was Gozem’s first data hire. As Global Data Analyst, he built the analytics function, led the migration to Google Cloud and BigQuery, and automated more than 50 analytical and operational workflows. He later led the data function as Head of Data, growing the team from 4 to 14+ and overseeing governance, FinOps, analytics engineering, and fintech data products. As Data Director through July 2026, he led multidisciplinary data teams supporting five African operating countries and co-led Gozem’s AI strategy.

## Data products at operating scale

Bolaji architected and led the development of the [Gozem Data Hub](https://www.bolablg.com/experience/gozem-director/#data-hub), a governed operational and analytics platform adopted by 700+ employees, field agents, and partners across five African countries. The published case documents React, FastAPI, AWS EC2, BigQuery, MongoDB, Nginx, Docker, Google Cloud Storage, Artifact Registry, and Python.

The data team monitored more than 250 pipelines. Bolaji established governance, observability, and reliability standards across 300+ workflow orchestration, transformation, and metrics-layer assets.

## Workload and measurement scope

At Gozem, batch workloads averaged approximately 5 TB of daily upserts into existing modeled tables. A separate streaming workload averaged 45 million telemetry and operational events per day; that event count was not all company activity and was not included in the batch measure. The platform contained nearly 300 Dataform models and Airflow DAGs combined, a different population from the 250+ monitored pipelines.

In the first month after deployment, recommendations were associated with approximately 12% of delivered orders.
