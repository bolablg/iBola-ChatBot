## How he works

Three principles run through everything above. **Metrics before dashboards**: he builds operational systems that act on metrics and patterns (automations, alerts, workflows) rather than reports that describe them. **Architecture is the budget**: cost discipline is designed into data models and pipelines, not bolted on afterwards. **Adoption is the deliverable**: a platform, strategy, or model only counts when the finance manager, the ops lead, and the executive are using it without the data team in the room.
