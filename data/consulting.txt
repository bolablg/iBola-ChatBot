BOLAJI BALOGOUN — CONSULTING EXPERIENCE

---

DATA MANAGEMENT CONSULTANT — International Trade Centre (ITC), United Nations Agency
Period: 2023 – 2024

Bolaji worked as a Data Management Consultant for the International Trade Centre (ITC), a joint agency of the United Nations and the World Trade Organization.

This engagement was part of the Netherlands Trust Fund V (NTF V) program, a partnership between the Dutch Ministry of Foreign Affairs and ITC.

Responsibilities and achievements:
- Guided startups across the ECOWAS (Economic Community of West African States) zone in leveraging data strategy to improve business operations and decision-making.
- Developed comprehensive training materials on data collection, data management, and data-driven decision-making.
- Delivered hands-on workshops for 15 startups from Benin and other ECOWAS countries.
- Provided one-on-one coaching to 5 startups, helping them address specific data management challenges and build internal data capabilities.

This role demonstrated Bolaji's ability to translate complex data concepts into actionable guidance for early-stage businesses in emerging markets.
