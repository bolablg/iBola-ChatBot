BOLAJI BALOGOUN - PREVIOUS ROLE: GLOBAL DATA ANALYST AT GOZEM

Status: PREVIOUS ROLE (November 2019 - September 2022)
Company: Gozem
Title: Global Data Analyst
Note: Bolaji was Gozem's FIRST DATA HIRE. He was promoted from this role to Head of Data in October 2022, then to Data Director in April 2025. His Gozem tenure ended in July 2026.

---

KEY ACHIEVEMENTS:

Migrated Gozem's data infrastructure to Google Cloud:
- Situation: the data infrastructure ran on legacy batch processes that could not keep up with growing operational and analytical needs across countries. There was no data warehouse. Bolaji had never worked with GCP or AWS before.
- Action: self-taught Google BigQuery, Cloud Storage, and related tools. Opted for a cloud-first approach to avoid maintenance overhead. Designed a BigQuery-based data warehouse with 50+ scheduled ETL pipelines.
- Tools: Google BigQuery, Google Cloud Storage, Google Dataflow, Apache Airflow, Python.
- Outcome: migrated 10+ TB within a single quarter, without operational interruption, into a fully functional cloud-based analytics infrastructure. Founded the analytics engineering function on top of it.

Automated operational monitoring:
- Situation: operations monitoring relied on a manual recurring job to spot business scenarios across markets, taking up to 2 days of analyst work per cycle.
- Action: automated the workflow within the first 6 months of the role. Python jobs querying PostgreSQL surfaced supply gaps, driver behaviour anomalies, and fraudulent trip patterns into Google Sheets for the operations team.
- Outcome: replaced a 2-day manual job with a 5-15 minute automated cycle.

Automated incentive payments:
- Situation: the agent and partner incentive payment process took multiple days, with manual reviews creating delays, errors, and heavy load on the finance team.
- Action: built automated data pipelines combining predictive analytics and near-real-time monitoring, with structured logs and dispute trails for finance and support.
- Outcome: reduced a multi-day manual process to minutes and facilitated more driver engagement.

Built the fraud patterns database:
- Situation: financial defaults from undetected fraud patterns were eroding margins, with no central knowledge base of known schemes.
- Action: implemented a fraud patterns database fed by automated daily collection monitoring, surfacing anomalies the same day they appeared.
- Outcome: zero unknown financial defaults during the period. Laid the groundwork for the later ML-based fraud screening pipeline.

Marketing segmentation and cohort analysis:
- Situation: marketing campaigns were broadly targeted because the team lacked a structured user profiling layer.
- Action: built a profiling database covering riders and drivers, with RFM segmentation and cohort analysis used by Marketing for targeted campaigns.
- Outcome: supported targeted campaigns and a 9%+ quarter-over-quarter increase in user retention.

Business health monitoring and daily insights:
- Situation: country managers and top management lacked a single daily lens on business health across markets.
- Action: consolidated 20+ KPIs into a daily report spanning all business areas, built in Google Sheets and transitioned to Google Data Studio and Tableau after the BigQuery migration.
- Outcome: daily insights presented to country managers and top management, surfacing trends and anomalies for operational decisions.

Built and led a distributed analytics team:
- Action: founded the analytics engineering function and grew a distributed team of 4, delivering automated dashboards, KPI reporting, and weekly market insights.

THE TRANSITION STORY:
Bolaji joined Gozem as its first data hire in 2019. He wore multiple hats (data analyst, cloud data engineer, analytics engineer, and data governance specialist) because there was no dedicated data engineering team. By teaching himself GCP and building the entire cloud infrastructure, he demonstrated the technical and leadership capability that led to his promotion to Head of Data in October 2022, and later to Data Director in April 2025.
