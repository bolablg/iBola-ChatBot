E-COMMERCE RECOMMENDATION DELIVERY AND POST-DEPLOYMENT MONITORING

Situation: Gozem's e-commerce business line needed personalized product recommendations to drive user engagement and sales.

Action: Bolaji oversaw an ML engineer's delivery of a recommendation feature in Gozem's customer mobile app and onboarded him to Vertex AI ML pipelines. The model pipeline used Vertex AI; Google Cloud Functions hosted the API integrated into the app. Bolaji collaborated with Product on event definitions and metric calculation, then monitored recommendation performance after deployment.

Events defined and tracked:
- User interaction events: clicks, views, and interactions with recommended products.
- Purchase events: conversions and purchases from recommendations.

Performance metrics:
- Click-Through Rate (CTR): percentage of users clicking on recommended products.
- Conversion rate: percentage of users purchasing recommended products.

Observed result: up to 12% of delivered orders were placed through the recommendation feature. This is observed order share, not a 12% incremental sales or conversion uplift. The measurement period and methodology are unspecified. Post-deployment monitoring is separate from the placement experiment below and does not by itself establish a causal effect.

Evidence: candidate-confirmed E-122 and E-139 in the Job Applications evidence ledger; distinction between placement experimentation and post-deployment monitoring clarified October 28, 2026. Team delivery and Bolaji's oversight/onboarding do not establish that he personally coded the recommendation model, API, or mobile integration.

RECOMMENDATION PLACEMENT EXPERIMENT

Situation: Product needed to decide where recommendations should appear in the customer app, comparing homepage placement, search placement, and a condition without recommendations.

Action: Bolaji collaborated with Product on the experiment's hypotheses, user sampling, event definitions, and metric calculation. The three conditions were no recommendations, recommendations on the homepage, and recommendations during search. His latest approximate recollection was 600 users in total.

Reported result: findings supported Product's decision to roll out homepage recommendations. No numerical lift, statistically significant result, exact sample count, experiment duration, or sole implementation ownership is established.

Evidence: E-139 and E-148, with the placement purpose clarified October 28, 2026. This experiment and the separate post-deployment monitoring answer different questions: where to place recommendations, and how the deployed feature performs.

MARKETING ANALYTICS STACK

Situation: growth reviews lacked a consistent analytical foundation across campaign platforms.

Action: Bolaji co-chartered the marketing analytics stack (CAC, LTV, cohort, and multi-touch attribution embedded into weekly Growth reviews), owning the end-to-end supporting data pipelines from campaign platforms.

Outcome: Marketing used a shared analytical layer for campaign and customer analysis. The approximately 9% retention improvement belongs to the separate seasonal driver-retention analysis, not to user retention or this marketing stack.
