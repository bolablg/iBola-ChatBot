ARTICLE 1: "Beyond standard Google Dataform: Smart templates to save BigQuery costs & Dev time"
Published: October 6, 2025

Bolaji describes how he extended Google Dataform beyond its basic capabilities by building a custom JavaScript-based template framework at Gozem. The framework automates the incremental data flow: table existence checks, staging, column comparison, dynamic partition extraction, partition-filtered merges, and scoped assertions. This approach achieved fully native data transformations inside BigQuery without external scripts or wasteful full-table scans, resulting in significant cost savings and faster development cycles. The work was done between mid-2024 and early 2025.

ARTICLE 2: "Google Sheets + Python: créer et partager automatiquement des rapports de données"
Published: May 31, 2025 (French)

A practical tutorial on automating the creation, population, formatting, and sharing of Google Sheets reports using Python. The business scenario covers daily compensation reports for sales agents based on performance metrics. Topics include MongoDB data extraction, Google Drive and Sheets API integration, automated formatting, and scheduling via cron.

Published: May 29, 2025 (French)

A step-by-step guide to creating Google Cloud service accounts for automation. Covers project creation in Google Cloud Console, service account setup, key generation, API enablement, Google Drive folder access configuration, and Python usage with the gspread library.

ARTICLE 4: "Où trouver des données pour une étude nationale?"
Published: November 6, 2021 (French)

A guide to finding national-level study data, using Benin as an example. Covers data sources including INStaD (national statistics institute), NADA portal, Benin Data Portal, Afrobaromètre, World Bank, DHS Program, ILOstat, and UNdata. Notes that Benin ranked 83rd globally on the Open Data Watch 2020 inventory with a score of 52/100.
