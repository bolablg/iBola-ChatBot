DATA SCIENTIST - RINTIO (July 2018 - May 2019)

Rintio is a technology company focused on precision agriculture. Bolaji developed analytics and data science products for Duniya, Rintio's smart-farming MVP for Beninese farmers, and supported outsourcing clients in their data transformation.

Key achievements:
- Designed and deployed a big data architecture combining a Hadoop data lake, an Apache Spark cluster, and ElasticSearch, ingesting 100GB+ of agricultural data from multiple sources (scraped market data, spreadsheets, PDFs, images) covering crops, fertilizers, yields, rainfall, and climate.
- Built end-to-end ingestion and transformation pipelines turning raw agricultural data into ML-ready features for the smart-farming app.
- Developed statistical crop yield forecast models using regression algorithms, mainly for maize with additional models for beans, achieving R squared above 80% in most cases. Forecasts powered sourcing, cycle, and pricing decisions.
- Delivered a consolidated leadership BI report in Kibana and Apache Superset covering multiple KPIs and data domains.
- Delivered an executive data & AI training mission to Societe Generale Maroc's top management: training content and lab use cases tailored to banking applications of data and AI.

Tools: Python, Apache Spark, Hadoop, ElasticSearch, scikit-learn, Kibana, Apache Superset, SQL, GitLab.
