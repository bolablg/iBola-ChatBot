REAL-TIME MARKET MONITORING SYSTEM — Transportation Operations

- Situation: Gozem's transportation operations needed real-time visibility into supply and demand during high-demand events such as public holidays, the Vodun Days festival, and major urban events.
- Action: Bolaji architected a multi-source real-time data pipeline aggregating GPS, booking, and driver availability signals with sub-minute refresh rates. He built dynamic driver redirection logic with rule-based and model-driven incentive triggers to balance supply across demand hotspots. He developed an operational dashboard with live demand density maps, supply gap indicators, driver engagement scores, and event-level KPIs. He implemented automated Google Chat alerts for supply-demand imbalances and extended champion inactivity.
- Tools: Python, BigQuery, Apache Airflow, Google Cloud Platform, Google Chat API, Looker Studio.
- Outcome: Validated during the Vodun Days festival with measurable improvement in response time and driver coverage in high-demand zones.
